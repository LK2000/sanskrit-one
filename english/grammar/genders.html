Genders (लिङ्गम्) in Sanskrit There are three genders in Sanskrit. Genders affect not only nouns but also adjectives, pronouns, and verbs.

पुल्लिङ्गः Masculine
Used for
  • Male beings
  • Some objects and abstract nouns
Examples:
  • रामः Rama
  • बालकः Boy
  • वृक्षः Tree
स्त्रीलिङ्गः Feminine
Used for
  • Female beings
  • Some objects and abstract nouns
  • Some nouns that are grammatically feminine
Examples:
  • सीता Sita
  • बालिका Girl
  • लता Creeper
  • नदी River
नपुंसकलिङ्गम् Neuter
Used for
  • Objects
  • Abstract concepts
  • Many inanimate things
Examples:
  • फलम् Fruit
  • जलम् Water
  • पुस्तकम् Book
How to Identify Gender In a majority of cases, the word ending indicates gender. But be careful because there are exceptions! When in doubt, use a dictionary

  • पुल्लिङ्गः words end in अ
  • स्त्रीलिङ्गः words end in आ or ई
  • नपुंसकलिङ्गम् words end in म्
