Pronouns सर्वनामानि



In Sanskrit grammar, a pronoun is called सर्वनाम. A सर्वनाम is a word that is used in place of a noun to avoid repetition and make sentences simpler and smoother.

For example, instead of repeating a person’s name again and again, Sanskrit uses words like:
  • सः He, that
  • सा She, that
  • तत्, It, that
Just as in English we say: “Rama is a boy. He studies.”
Sanskrit says: रामः बालकः अस्ति। सः पठति।

The word सर्वनाम literally means: “a word standing in place of all nouns.”
These words can refer to:
  • Persons
  • Objects
  • Places
  • Ideas
Types of Pronouns in Sanskrit
Sanskrit has several kinds of सर्वनाम-s.

1. Personal Pronouns
Used for persons. Examples:
  • अहम् I
  • त्वम् You
  • वयम् We
2. Demonstrative सर्वनाम-s
Used to point out persons or objects. Examples:
  • सः That, he
  • एषः This, he
3. Interrogative सर्वनाम-s
Used in questions. Examples:
  • कः Who? (masculine)
  • का Who? (feminine)
  • किम् What? (neuter)
4. Relative सर्वनाम-s
Used in relative clauses. Examples:
  • यः Who, which (masculine)
  • या Who, which (feminine)
  • यत् Which, that (neuter)
सर्वनाम-s Change According to Gender and Number
Like nouns, Sanskrit सर्वनाम-s change according to:
  • लिङ्गम्
  • वचनम्
  • विभक्तिः (case)
सर्वनाम-s and Agreement
सर्वनाम-s must agree with the nouns they represent. Examples:
  • रामः पठति। सः बुद्धिमान् अस्ति। “Rama studies. He is intelligent.”
  • सीता गच्छति। सा प्रसन्ना अस्ति। “Sita goes. She is happy.”
सर्वनाम-s are among the most frequently used words in Sanskrit literature and daily expressions.

Interrogative सर्वनाम-s - Asking questions in Sanskrit
Remember that in Sanskrit, सर्वनाम-s have to match in लिङ्ग and वचन.

Interrogative सर्वनाम-s for दूरे: (far)

एकवचनम् द्विवचनम् बहुवचनम्
पुल्लिङ्गः सः कः तौ कौ ते के
Who is that Who are those two Who are they
स्त्रीलिङ्गः सा का ते के ताः काः
Who is she Who are those two Who are they
नपुंसकलिङ्गम् तत् किम् ते के तानि कानि
What is that What are those two What are they

एकवचनम् द्विवचनम् बहुवचनम्
पुल्लिङ्गः एषः कः एतौ कौ एते के
Who is this Who are these two Who are these
स्त्रीलिङ्गः एषा का एते के एताः काः
Who is she Who are these two Who are these
नपुंसकलिङ्गम् एतत् किम् एते के एतानि कानि
