अव्ययानि (Avyayāni) — Indeclinable Words

Most words in Sanskrit change their form based on gender (लिङ्ग), number (वचन), or case (विभक्ति). For example, when vibhakti is applied to a word, it can have as many as 24 forms.

Avyayas (अव्यय singluar, अव्ययानि plural) are a category of words that do not change their form regardless of लिङ्ग, वचन or विभक्ति. The word अव्यय literally means "unchanging" or "invariable."

Characteristics of an अव्यय
  • Has only one form
  • Does not take case endings (विभक्तयः)
  • Does not change for singular, dual, or plural
  • Does not change according to masculine, feminine, or neuter gender
  • Usually modifies a verb, an adjective, or an entire sentence
Importance of अव्ययानि
Although अव्ययानि do not change their form, they are essential for expressing time, place, manner, emphasis, connection, and emotion. They make sentences more natural, precise, and expressive. A good knowledge of अव्ययानि greatly improves both Sanskrit reading and conversation.
